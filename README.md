## 🌟 Razorpay-Specific AI Risk & Governance Features

* **💳 Fintech PII & Token Scrubber**: Real-time detection and automatic masking of Indian payment identifiers including **UPI VPAs** (`name@okhdfcbank`), **Indian PAN** (`ABCDE1234F`), **Aadhaar IDs**, Credit Card PANs, CVVs, and **Razorpay Live API Keys** (`rzp_live_...`).
* **🛑 Autonomous Refund & Limit Trapper**: Blocks adversarial prompt injections and DAN-mode exploits attempting to force AI customer support bots to issue unauthorized refunds or manipulate credit limits without merchant authorization.
* **📋 RBI & PCI-DSS Model Auditor**: Automates compliance audits against **RBI Master Directions on Cyber Security / Digital Payment Security Controls**, **PCI-DSS v4.0**, **EU AI Act**, and **ISO/IEC 42001**, generating official printable certificates signed with **AMIT KUSHWAHA's verified stamp**.
* **🚨 Attack & Webhook Simulator**: Interactive stress-testing lab for Razorpay Webhook HMAC spoofing, RAG merchant underwriting vector poisoning, and credit scoring model inversion.
* **🎬 5-Minute Interactive Video Pitch Stage (With Voice & 1-Click Video Downloader)**: Integrated presentation stage with **live AI Speech Synthesis voiceover** and a direct **MediaRecorder video exporter** (`video-pitch.html`) to record and download the pitch video with voice.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or serve via local HTTP server:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Or using Node.js
npx http-server -p 8080
```

2. Navigate to `http://localhost:8080/`.

## 🎙️ How to Watch & Download the 5-Minute Pitch Video with Voice

1. Open `video-pitch.html` in your browser.
2. The presentation will **automatically narrate each slide aloud** using browser Speech Synthesis voiceover.
3. Click **"🔴 Record & Download Video Pitch (with Voice)"** at the top right:
   - Select your current tab/screen to record high-definition visuals with audio.
   - Sentinel AI will automatically record the pitch and generate a downloadable `.webm` / `.mp4` video file!
4. You can also click **"Download Voice Script"** to export the verbatim teleprompter audio script.
